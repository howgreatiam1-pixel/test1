WealthOS privacy

WealthOS stores your financial records only on your device, in your browser's storage. Each profile is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your passphrase. We do not operate servers that receive your data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What we cannot do: we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase or a lost backup passphrase. Clearing your browser data erases the app data stored on that device unless you have an encrypted backup.

Backups and exports: encrypted backups are protected by the passphrase you choose. CSV exports are not encrypted; keep them private.

Network use: the app works offline after the first visit. It makes no requests to third parties.
